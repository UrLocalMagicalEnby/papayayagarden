# Story

Ceruleum was born in community Z of Xokera. He had a single father after his mother committed suicide after her birth. The father, like most other men, had previously raped his mom and stayed with her until she had the baby. Afterward, he announced that he was leaving her, seeing as her purpose had been fulfilled in his eyes. This caused her to kill herself because she felt like she had no support in experiencing her events.

The earlier years were hard on Ceruleum's father because he did not know how to raise a child. In order to help, he brought his family friend. By the time Ceruleum was 5, the family friend was closer to her father, now being her step-mother. She, however, had her own struggles which she didn't reveal to her husband, like her substance abuse. At the same time, she was insanely attracted to Ceruleum, making comments about her outfit or asking her to bend over to pick something up while wearing extremely short skirts. Eventually, her attraction reached a certain level where she could not leave it alone. One night, she brought a group of friends over while her dad wasn't home, and they all took turns raping her. Since they were medicated and couldn't handle emotion, they beat her whenever she cried or begged for them to stop. Similar to her biological mother, she felt unsupported because no one believed she was raped, including her father and mother.

Realizing Ceruleum didn't have anyone to confide in, she slowly stopped caring about life. She stopped caring for herself, physically and in beauty, to the point where her hair was matted and she herself was pale. She was worsening behaviourally as well, not leaving for school or doing chores out of anxiety. From the trauma, she developed selective mutism, meaning she couldn't talk in situations giving her anxiety, such as seeing her abusers, her parents. Of course, her actions had consequences. Whenever she showed her face, her parents called her disgusting, or compared her to various animals. If she didn't behave how she wanted, her parents would beat her.

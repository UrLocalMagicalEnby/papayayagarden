# Story

Crystal came from Community A, Xokera, as a result of her father raping her mother. They decided to stay together because they otherwise loved each other, and their personalities matched. However, both of them were extremely abusive, with the father being a pedophile and the mother being emotionally abusive.

When Crystal was 5, her father attempted to sexually assault her. Although mostly successful, she woke up while being fingered. The following morning, she attempted to tell her mother, but she denied the events and accused her of being ungrateful. This was the response she received for many other things, like being bullied. 

Whenever something good happened to her, her mother took the spotlight, claiming that it was only because of her she would achieve it. If she got mad over it, her mother would either kick her out for the night, or threaten to kill herself and her father. 

Crystal eventually learned to stop telling her family anything about her, isolating herself from her family. The fear of being rejected or ignored led her to have social anxiety that would further her isolation. The thought of going outside caused her to have a panic attack, which was only worsened by her mother calling her crazy and worthless.**

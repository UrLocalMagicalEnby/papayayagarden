# Story

Don was born in Community F alongside [Swen](Swen), next to the government's headquarters. With his parents a part of the government, he was very over-privileged, getting whatever he wanted whenever he wanted. His parents allotted unlimited money and love for their son, so they fulfilled his wishes. If they didn't, he would throw a tantrum, destroying valuable objects. He responded to no forms of punishment, only praise. So, he spent his childhood spoiled. As well as this, he was the epitome of masculine expectations of the time, going after any person. At its worst, he would kidnap children and rape them. He was never punished, however, due to his parents' status.

It wasn't a one sided trade though. In return for riches, Don was taught everything about the gods, essentially brainwashing him. He was sent out into the world to convince the people to love the gods. This was difficult because many people suffered from abuse or loss, leaving most of Don's efforts useless.

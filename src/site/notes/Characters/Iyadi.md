# Story

Iyadi was born in an alleyway in Community R. Her first two years of life were just as average as anyone at the time. That was, until her parents died mysteriously not too long after her third birthday. Upon her parents' death, she was sent to a government facility that cares for orphans like her. However, it was severely underfunded, with commodities often being inaccessible. At its worst, there was no food, supplies, clothes, nor staff. The staff that did work there were mainly perverts, making everyone wear worn down clothes that were transparent and short. The kids living there essentially started an anarchy where everybody lives for themselves. Many of them would leave during the night to gather items, such as fabric, food, or drugs, and trade them off to the kids living there.

Iyadi was one of the traders, although not for drugs, but for fabric. She would go to the market and buy a loaf of bread, cut it up, and give it to kids for money and fabric. Once she had enough, she would make various garments, mainly skirts. She made mostly skirts because the boys there, like the staff, were undisciplined and pervy, sometimes raping the other younger girls as punishment. This made Iyadi become assertive, sometimes going out to teach herself self-defense. She was taught to be honest and defensive, to fight if needed, and to say exactly what she means.

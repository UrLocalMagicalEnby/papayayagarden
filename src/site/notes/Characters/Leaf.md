# Story

Leaf was born in Community F, Xokera to a single mom. His childhood was similar to that of everyone around him, including the fact he was in poverty. He rarely had two meals a day, let alone three. Doctors considered him to be malnourished, which caused him frequent fainting and sickness, but he otherwise behaved normally.

This is, until he was 5, when he was left alone at home. A man, about thirty years of age, saw him wearing a skirt and assumed he was a girl. Once Leaf spotted him, he opened the door because he was his uncle. He was under the assumption that he had come by to babysit him. This, however, caused him to be sexually abused for 6 months, his uncle threatening him with physical assault if he resisted. Afterward, he lost interest in him and never came back to the house, although appearing in some family gatherings.

The year after that, Leaf was defiant, never wanting to listen to anybody nor visit his family. At its worst, he would spiral into anger outbursts, refusing to talk to his mother while attempting to kill himself. When this happened, his mother beat him with a metal container to get him to behave. He stopped defying others in fear they would beat him as well. 

He tries to be a good example to the gods and others in hopes that we would be spared from the pain he experienced from the trauma.  However, he had mostly forgotten the events, leaving him stuck the same way in fear.

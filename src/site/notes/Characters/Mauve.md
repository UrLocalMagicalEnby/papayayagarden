# Story

Mauve was born in community B, Xokera. Her mother had died giving birth to her, so she lived with her father and grandfather, the grandmother already deceased. Due to the actions of the king, Mauve was forced to become a boy in order to prevent herself from being raped. She received multiple invasive surgeries in order to almost pass as a man, including removing her uterus. Her dad manipulated her into getting these surgeries, with him telling her that this is the only way she can be loved.

Being loved was hard for her, as, unlike most other families who grouped together in spite of the king, hers grew distant. Oftentimes, Mauve had to prove to her caretakers that she was worthy of love. This allowed her father to take advantage of her, cutting off friendships he didn't approve of, making her hurt, rape, or otherwise abuse other children, forcing her to be a bully, all to fit the gender roles for men at the time. By the end, her and her family were well hated, receiving death threats next to every day.

When she was ten, Mauve started hating her father and grandfather. She hid everything from them, like the friends she made or the grades she got in school. However, once she was found out, her grandfather would beat her for three hours straight. Once, her father told her to leave if she didn't like her home after she confided in him about how much pain she was in. From there, she decided to leave home permanently and move to the city in Community T. She packed her clothes, books, and enough food to last her three days, and took a train to the city. She separated herself from others, only talking to strangers when absolutely needed.

At one point, she attempted to kill herself after a particularly rough night when she was beat up by a group of kids. She stood in the street and waited to be run over. Instead, however, a stranger found her right as she was about to be hit and took her to the government, where she was placed in an orphanage. Much like the streets, she isolated herself from other people, refusing to talk to any of the other kids, thus going back to her old ways.

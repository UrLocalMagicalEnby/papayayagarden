# Story

Rosemary was born in Community W, Xokera. Her parents conceived her accidentally at a party and decided to stay together to not burden the other with the child. Despite this, they were decent parents. Although they only got the bare minimum for Rosemary, she was still satisfied. After all, if she wasn't, her parents would gaslight her into being so. Other than this, she felt loved and like a part of the family. This love only grew over time, with the parents starting to get used to being together with each other and her. Despite this, the parents knew they weren't a good match, but if they split, Rosemary would have to pick a side. Despite them doing so eventually, they wanted to give their relationship a shot, no matter the cost.

On Rosemary's tenth birthday, one of the presents she opened was empty, which happened to be the one from her father. Her parents, having embarrassed themselves in front of their entire family, started having a fight that lasted for almost thirty minutes. It was only stopped when Rosemary tried to show her appreciation for the attempt, but this caused everyone to yell at her. This kind of situation, where somebody was let down and the parents fought, happened almost every week from then on. Whether it was forgetting the bills or not completing a chore, the parents would start an intolerably long shouting match. To combat them, Rosemary would often listen in and try to find the solution to the problem they had. She found herself to be best for this because at that time, she was the only one who had enough clarity to think of a solution. This brought satisfaction to her parents, solidifying her as a people pleaser.

By the time Rosemary was twelve, she was sick of her parents fighting. This was worsened by the fact the only way to make them stop was to put herself in the way and provide a logical solution. However, the problem was only just beginning. Her parents decided to divorce, and wanted her for full custody. Behind the other's back, her parents would gaslight her into liking one over the other. At its worst, her parents would put their entire lives or their love for her on choosing them over the other. Ultimately, she opted for split custody, letting each parent see her. Her parents still complained about the other whenever she was around, almost like a last ditch effort.

# Story

Tyen was born in Community D in Xokera. They lived with their parents and older brother. While their brother was born a boy, they were born a girl. Due to the sexual violence women received at the time, Tyen was taught self defense by their parents. To be extra safe, Tyen was disguised as a boy in order to be safer in public. While teaching them self defense, their parents passed on their beliefs to Tyen, allowing them to always support their parents.

When Tyen was five, her brother came out as a girl. Their parents rejected them because they wanted them to be safe in public. She tried multiple times, but her parents refused to let her transition. Because Tyen had the same beliefs as their parents, their sister had nobody to vent to, and therefore no support. They eventually committed suicide, with Tyen finding out first. Tyen realized that it would be better to let somebody be who they are, causing them to be more hospitable and adaptive than before.

To support their parents in their grief, Tyen worked different jobs. Their favorite was surveying, where they would go from house to house and ask questions, and interpret the numbers. At the same time, they also participated in the arts, including but not limited to, theater, painting, and writing. As they grew older, they had a love for writing, whether it be scenes, descriptions, or anything. The police found them helpful in cases for their ability to piece together a story through multiple accounts, making their job already sealed. They enjoyed the feeling of being depended on, and strove for it and any social situation, becoming the leader.
